Test your stream with Wi-Fi off

Turn Wi-Fi off in your phone's Settings when you test at home, so the test runs on mobile data like your stream outside.

How

  1. Turn Wi-Fi off in your phone's Settings. Leave mobile data on.
  2. Watch in your hub's preview without going live, or on a Private YouTube stream with nobody invited.
  3. Watch it on a second device. Mute it, or you'll hear yourself twice.

Check: the picture keeps arriving on the second device while the phone's Wi-Fi is off.

  • Watch out: On iPhone, the Wi-Fi button in Control Center only disconnects you from the network. Turn Wi-Fi off in Settings.
  • A hotspot or a spare phone reaches your phone over Wi-Fi. Test those with each connection on its own.
