Frozen on stream? Look at your own preview

Viewers see a frozen picture? Look at the preview on the phone you stream from while something moves in front of it.

How

  1. Look at the preview on the phone you stream from, while something in front of it moves.
  2. Frozen there too? Check the camera, its connection to the phone, and the app.
  3. Still moving? Check what your app is sending.

Check: still moving on your phone? Your app still gets a picture. Check what it sends, then your connection and the next stop in your setup.

  • Viewers are a few seconds behind you, and a still view can look frozen. Wave, or point the camera at something that moves.
