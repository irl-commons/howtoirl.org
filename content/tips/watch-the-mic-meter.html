Say one line and watch the mic meter

Say one normal sentence and watch the mic meter in your app. The bar should move with your words.

How

  1. Open your app with the scene you'll stream.
  2. Make sure the app has picked the mic you want.
  3. Say one normal sentence and watch the meter.

Check: the bar moves with your words, so that mic is picking you up. If it stays flat, check which mic is selected.

  • It checks the mic going into your app, not the sound further along.
  • Watch out: A scene can switch to another mic. In Moblin that's a mic override, so check again after you swap mics or scenes.
