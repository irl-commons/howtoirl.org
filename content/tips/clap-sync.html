Clap once to check your sound is in sync

Clap once on camera. On your second device, you should hear the clap when your hands meet.

How

  1. Watch in your hub's preview without going live, or on a Private YouTube stream with nobody invited.
  2. Use headphones on the second device.
  3. Clap once in front of the camera. Watch and listen on the second device.

Check: if the clap sounds early or late on that screen, your sound is out of sync.

  • It shows the sync on the screen you watch, not where it slips.
