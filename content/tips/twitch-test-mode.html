Test on Twitch without going live

Add ?bandwidthtest=true after your stream key. Twitch takes the stream but nobody can watch it, so you can check it in Twitch Inspector first.

How

  1. Put ?bandwidthtest=true right after the stream key. In an app that takes the whole stream URL in one field, like Moblin, it goes at the very end: rtmp://<server>/app/<stream key>?bandwidthtest=true.
  2. Start the stream. Twitch doesn't show it to viewers.
  3. Open Twitch Inspector, log in, and check the bitrate and whether the connection stays stable.
  • Watch out: Take ?bandwidthtest=true out again before the real stream, or nobody will see it.
Sources

Twitch: Broadcast URLs and stream keys ("an optional query-string parameter that disables live viewing of the stream", and the URL format rtmp://<ingest-server>/app/<stream-key>[?bandwidthtest=true]), Twitch Inspector (bitrate, and stable or unstable). Moblin: its RTMP URL check, which refuses a URL without the stream key.
