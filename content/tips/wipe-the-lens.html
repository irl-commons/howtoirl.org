Hazy picture? Wipe the lens

A smudge on the lens can make lights glow and blur. Wipe it with a clean lens cloth before you change a setting.

How

  1. In your app's preview, look at a light.
  2. Gently wipe the lens of the camera you stream with, using a clean lens cloth.
  3. Look at the same view again, in the same light.

Check: same view, clearer? Then a smudge was likely part of it.

  • It only helps when dirt is the cause. Compare with the same camera, in the same light.
  • Watch out: Grit on the glass? Check the camera maker's cleaning advice before you wipe.

Sources

Apple: if the camera or flash isn't working ("If photos are blurry, clean the front and back camera lenses with a microfiber cloth"). Google: Pixel photo or video quality ("dirt on the camera lens can cause blur, halos"; "Gently wipe the camera lens and its surrounding glass with a soft, clean, and lint-free cloth"). GoPro: MAX manual, Maintenance ("To clean the lenses, wipe them with a soft, lint-free cloth").
