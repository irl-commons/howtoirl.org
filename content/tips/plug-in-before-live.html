Plug everything in before you go live

Some multiport power banks cut power for a moment when you plug in or unplug a second device. Plug everything in first, then go live.

How

  1. Keep the stream off. Plug in your first device as usual.
  2. Plug your second device into the same bank.
  3. Watch the first one. Does its charging icon or power light blink, or does it restart?

Check: everything in and running? Check your gear is back up, then go live.

  • Only some multiport banks do this. A phone with its own battery may ride through it, and the cut can be too quick to see, so plug in first anyway.
