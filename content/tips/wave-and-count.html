Wave and count to see your delay

Wave at the camera and count the seconds until the wave shows up on your second device. That's roughly your delay to that screen.

How

  1. Watch in your hub's preview without going live, or on a Private YouTube stream with nobody invited.
  2. Hold the second device where you can see it.
  3. Wave at the camera and count the seconds.

Check: stop when you see the wave. That's roughly your delay to that screen, in seconds.

  • It's a rough number for that one screen. Your viewers watch somewhere else, so their delay can differ.
