Test your power bank with the camera idle

Some power banks shut off when a camera uses very little power. Check that yours keeps powering the camera while it sits idle.

How

  1. Plug your camera into the bank.
  2. Leave the camera on but not recording. Does it keep getting power?
  3. Lost power? Turn on the bank's low-current mode, if it has one.

Check: with low-current mode on, leave the camera idle again. Does it keep getting power?

  • Not every bank has the mode, and how you turn it on differs. On Anker banks, the mode turns itself off after 2 hours if you don't touch the bank.
